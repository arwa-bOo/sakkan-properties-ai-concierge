District: 6th of October

Neighbourhood guide for 6th of October (October Gardens and the Wahat Road compounds).
Commute: 45-70 minutes to Downtown depending on the Mehwar road traffic.
Schools: Good private schools; fewer international options.
Malls and lifestyle: Several family malls and Hosary Square.
Average price: about 24,000 EGP per sqm for apartments.
