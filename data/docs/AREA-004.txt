Neighbourhood guide for Maadi (Degla, Sarayat and the Road 9 area).
Commute: 20-35 minutes to Downtown by car or metro.
Schools: Strong international school cluster and many nurseries.
Malls and lifestyle: Maadi City Center and boutique shops on Road 9.
Average price: about 45,000 EGP per sqm for apartments.
