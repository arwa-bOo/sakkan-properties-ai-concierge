Neighbourhood guide for New Cairo (Fifth Settlement and surrounding compounds).
Commute: 35-50 minutes to Downtown outside rush hour, 25 minutes to Cairo Airport.
Schools: Several international schools including Nile International School and Horizon American School.
Malls and lifestyle: Large malls and the 90th Street cafe strip.
Average price: about 33,000 EGP per sqm for apartments.
