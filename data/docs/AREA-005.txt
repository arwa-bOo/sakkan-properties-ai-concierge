Neighbourhood guide for North Coast (Sidi Abdel Rahman to Ras El Hekma strip).
Commute: 3 to 4 hours drive from Cairo.
Schools: Seasonal; no permanent international schools.
Malls and lifestyle: Beach clubs, marina restaurants and seasonal malls.
Average price: about 38,000 EGP per sqm for apartments.
