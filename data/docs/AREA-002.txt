District: Sheikh Zayed

Neighbourhood guide for Sheikh Zayed (Zayed City and the Beverly Hills area).
Commute: 40-60 minutes to Downtown, direct access to the Desert Road.
Schools: Established private and international schools within 15 minutes.
Malls and lifestyle: Zayed Plaza Mall and several open-air shopping streets.
Average price: about 31,000 EGP per sqm for apartments.
